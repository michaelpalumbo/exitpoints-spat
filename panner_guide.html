Quad panner: operator guide

Created by Michael Palumbo, Nov 2026, for Exit Points #78

You move the instruments around the four speakers and set their levels. FOH handles gain, EQ and the backup PA.

Words used in this guide

UnitOne instrument (or one player's mics) that moves as a single sound. Up to 8 per set, one per nanoKONTROL2 strip.
SceneOne set's list of units, with their levels and settings: ensemble 1, ensemble 2, or switchemups 1-4.
ModeA way of moving, picked with the number keys (drift, orbit, hop...).
Movable / frozenA movable unit follows the mode. A frozen unit stays where it is. Switched with M.
HomeA unit's resting place in the room, set in mode 1 (set home positions). R sends a unit there from any other mode.
PickupA knob or fader does nothing until you move it past its current value, so nothing jumps.
IN numberThe console channel(s) a unit uses, shown in its box (IN:5/6). Use it when talking to FOH.
Glide / cutWhether changes happen smoothly or instantly. TAB switches.
Line-upThe units chosen for a switchemups set.

Golden rules

Keep the Max window in front, or the number keys won't work.

Every unit starts frozen. Nothing moves until you press its M button.

Frozen and movable are kept separately for each mode. A unit you made movable in orbit is still frozen in drift until you press its M there too. When you switch modes, each unit goes back to however you left it in that mode.

The one exception is mode 1, set home positions: there every unit is always movable (all M buttons lit, and M does nothing), so each unit sits at its home and you can drag it to a new one.

Everything saves itself. You never need to save the patch.

Before each set

  1. Load the scene with the scene buttons (ensemble 1, ensemble 2, or switchemups 1-4).
  2. Check the 8 unit boxes at the bottom of the room view: each one shows its name and its input channels (for example IN:5/6).
  3. Pick a mode with the number keys, then press M on the units you want moving.

nanoKONTROL2

One channel strip per unit: strip 1 is unit 1, and so on.

FaderUnit level. Top is full level (0 dB), the very bottom is off.
MMake the unit movable or frozen. Lit = movable. In set home positions, all are lit and M does nothing.
RSend the unit to its home (the one set in mode 1). R flashes while it travels.
M off: it stays home. M on: it goes straight back to what the mode is doing (in loop mode, back onto its loop).
Does nothing in set home positions, where every unit is already home.
S (tap)In assign mode only: select the unit (it flashes slowly). Tap again to deselect.
S (hold 2 s)Delete the unit's loop. It flashes fast while you hold. Let go early to keep the loop.
KnobsWhat they change depends on the transport button you last pressed (below).
ButtonKnobs change
<<spread: from a point to a wide, blurry sound (the halo in the room view grows)
>>width: how far apart a stereo pair or mic group sits. Single mics show "n/a (mono)".
STOPrange: how far the unit travels from home. 0% stays home.
PLAYspeed: how fast the unit moves
RECaccel: at the top it moves instantly, lower and it moves with weight
CYCLEglobal: knob 1 overall speed, knob 2 glide time

Range, speed and accel are remembered separately for each mode. Level, spread and width are the same in every mode.

In set home positions, range, speed and accel do nothing, so their buttons are greyed out and their knobs show "n/a".

A knob or fader does nothing? That's pickup. Move it until it passes the value shown on the screen (the line or dot), then it takes over. This stops levels from jumping.

Keyboard

1 set home positionsEvery unit sits at its home. Drag a unit in the room view to set a new home. That's where R sends it in every other mode.
2 driftSlow wandering around the room.
3 orbitCircling the middle of the room.
4 hopJumps between speakers at random.
5 pulseJumps between speakers together, on a beat.
6 swarmClustered together, travelling as a group.
7 swayFront of the room to the back and back again.
8 perimeterSpread around the walls, slowly circling.
9 loopDrag a unit in the room view to draw a path. It loops when you let go.
0 assignEach unit runs its own mode. Tap S on units, then press a number to give them that mode.
TABGlide (smooth changes) or cut (instant changes). Shown top right of the room view.

Switchemups: create or edit a set

Do this in the second intermission, with the sign-up sheet. Each of the 4 sets is its own scene.

  1. Load switchemups 1 with the scene buttons.
  2. Click the blue EDIT LINE-UP button at the top of the room view. It turns orange and says DONE.
  3. Click unit box 1 at the bottom. A list opens.
  4. Click the instrument or input for that player. Each entry shows its channels (IN:15).
    Grey entries are already used by another unit (it says which one).
    Someone on a channel that isn't a named instrument? Pick the IN # entry for that channel.
  5. Repeat for each player (up to 8). Pick (empty) to clear a slot.
  6. Click DONE.
  7. Load switchemups 2 and repeat, then 3 and 4.

Then for each set: load it, set the levels with the faders, and press 1 (set home positions) to drag each unit to roughly where that player stands. All of this is remembered.

Start a set over from scratch: press its erase button. It empties the line-up and forgets its levels and positions. Only switchemups sets can be erased.

A problem with an input? Tell FOH the IN number from the unit box.

Monitoring switch

Three buttons that choose where the sound goes.

SettingUse it for
Array Routing - Direct-In MonitoringThe show. The four speakers play the panned sound. Your headphones get every unit at its level, without the panning, so you can judge balance from your seat.
Practice (Laptop) - Direct-in MonitoringPractising on your laptop: you hear every unit at its level, without the panning. Good for setting levels.
Practice (Laptop) - Post-Pan MonitoringPractising on your laptop: you hear the panning folded down to stereo. Left and right movement comes through, front and back doesn't.

Before the show, check it's on Array Routing. The practice settings send sound to the laptop, not to the speakers.

If something goes wrong

Something too loudPull that unit's fader down (it picks up first, see above).
Too much movementPress M to freeze units, then R to send them home.
Keys do nothingClick the Max patch window so it's in front.
No sound at all from the quadCheck the monitoring switch is on Array Routing. If it is, tell FOH straight away: they bring up the backup PA.
